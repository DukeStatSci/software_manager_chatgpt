# Guide for obtaining and accessing Duke ChatGPT Edu

This guide describes how eligible Duke University users can obtain the
no-cost **ChatGPT - Duke Standard** license through Duke's Software Manager and
then sign in to ChatGPT using Duke's Shibboleth single sign-on (SSO).

ChatGPT Edu is Duke's managed, privacy-focused ChatGPT workspace. OpenAI does
not use data entered in the Duke workspace to train its models. The service is
designed for responsible use with Duke sensitive and protected data, but **not
Protected Health Information (PHI)**. It is not currently available for Duke
Health or Duke Kunshan University. See Duke OIT's [ChatGPT Edu service
page](https://oit.duke.edu/service/chatgpt-edu/) for the current service scope
and restrictions.

The steps below were last checked on **August 27, 2026**. Duke's [August 24,
2026 service
announcement](https://oit.duke.edu/news/expanded-chatgpt-edu-now-available-duke-no-cost/)
states that the enhanced Standard offering is available at no cost, requires
no fund code, and is subject to usage limits.

## Before you begin

You will need:

* An active, eligible Duke University NetID.
* Access to Duke's Shibboleth login and multi-factor authentication process.
* To be at least 18 years old. The Software Manager order form requires you to
  confirm your age and accept the service terms.

Use your canonical Duke address, **`netid@duke.edu`**, throughout setup. This
may differ from the preferred email alias that you normally give to other
people. Using the canonical address helps the ChatGPT account, Software Manager
license, and Duke Shibboleth identity match correctly.

## Obtain the license in Software Manager

1. Open [Duke Software Manager](https://software-manager.oit.duke.edu/) and log
   in with your Duke NetID. Complete multi-factor authentication if prompted.

2. On the **Search for Software** page, find **ChatGPT - Duke Standard** in the
   list of available software and select its name. The price should be listed
   as **Free**.

   <img src="sm-landing.png" alt="Duke Software Manager Search for Software page with ChatGPT - Duke Standard listed as free" width="100%" />

3. Review the product description and eligibility information. When ready,
   select **Order Software**.

   <img src="sm-chatgpt.png" alt="ChatGPT - Duke Standard product details page in Duke Software Manager" width="100%" />

4. Read and accept the terms presented on the order page, including the age
   confirmation and restrictions on use, and complete the order. The current
   Standard offering does not require a fund code.

5. Open the **My Licenses** tab and confirm that **ChatGPT - Duke Standard**
   appears under **Current Licenses**. Select **View details** if you need to
   review the license information.

   <img src="sm-licenses.png" alt="Duke Software Manager My Licenses page showing a current ChatGPT - Duke Standard license" width="100%" />

If the **Order Software** button is disabled and the page says that you are
already licensed, you do not need to order again. If a completed order does not
appear immediately under **My Licenses**, refresh the page or wait a few minutes
while the assignment is processed. These steps follow Duke OIT's general
[Software Manager ordering instructions](https://oit.duke.edu/help/articles/kb0035630/).

## Sign in to ChatGPT with Duke Shibboleth SSO

Once the license appears under **My Licenses**:

1. In a web browser, go to [chatgpt.com](https://chatgpt.com/) and select **Log
   in**. Starting in a browser is the simplest way to complete the initial
   account setup, even if you plan to use a mobile or desktop app later.

2. Enter **`netid@duke.edu`**, replacing `netid` with your own NetID. Do not use
   a preferred Duke email alias or a personal email address for the Duke
   workspace.

3. Select the option to continue with SSO. ChatGPT should redirect you to Duke's 
   Shibboleth login page. Enter your Duke NetID credentials and complete multi-factor authentication.

4. Review any OpenAI onboarding, workspace, or managed-account notice that is
   displayed. When setup is complete, open the profile menu and confirm that
   you are using the Duke-managed workspace rather than a separate personal
   account.


## If you already used ChatGPT with your Duke email

Read the migration screen carefully before making a selection. If an existing
personal ChatGPT account uses the same `netid@duke.edu` address, OpenAI may ask
you to move that account into Duke's managed ChatGPT Edu workspace. Depending
on the options presented, you may be able to:

* Transfer existing chat history and GPTs into the Duke workspace; or
* Export the personal account's data, delete that personal workspace, and begin
  with an empty Duke workspace.

This migration is consequential: once the personal workspace is removed, it
cannot simply be restored as it was. Export and verify a copy of anything you
need **before** completing the migration. OpenAI's documentation explains the
[existing-account migration
flow](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/10479654)
and how to [export ChatGPT data before moving to a managed
workspace](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/7260999-how-do-i-export-my-chatgpthistory-and-data).
OpenAI states that a paid personal subscription associated with a migrated
account is automatically canceled, but you should still confirm the
subscription status and any app-store billing after migration.

An existing personal ChatGPT account registered to a non-Duke address is
separate and should not need to be migrated.

## Data and continued access

Content created in the Duke workspace is governed by Duke's agreement,
workspace settings, and retention policies. Although Duke states that workspace
data is not used to train OpenAI's models, this is an institution-managed
account rather than a private personal account. Do not enter PHI, and follow
Duke policies and any course, research, or departmental requirements that
apply to your work.

Access is also tied to your eligible Duke affiliation. In particular, Duke's
[guidance for departing students](https://oit.duke.edu/help/articles/kb0023279/)
states that ChatGPT access ends when student affiliation is removed and cannot
be restored. Before leaving Duke, save any material you are permitted to retain.
Whether self-service export is available within an Edu workspace depends on
the workspace's settings and your assigned permissions; see OpenAI's [ChatGPT
Edu export documentation](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001279-exporting-data-from-a-chatgpt-edu-workspace).

## Troubleshooting

* **The license is not listed:** Return to Software Manager, check **My
  Licenses**, and refresh after a few minutes. Make sure the order was completed
  rather than left at the product-details page.
* **ChatGPT does not send you to Duke Shibboleth SSO:** Sign out of all ChatGPT
  accounts and begin again with the exact `netid@duke.edu` address. A private
  browser window can help rule out a cached personal session.
* **The Duke workspace is missing:** Confirm that the Standard license appears
  under **My Licenses**, then sign out and sign in again. Do not delete and
  recreate an existing OpenAI account merely to resolve an SSO or workspace
  mismatch.
* **An existing account or history appears to be missing:** Check which email
  address and workspace are selected in the profile menu. If the email returned
  by SSO differs from the address on the existing account, contact support
  rather than creating another account.
* **The problem continues:** Contact the [Duke OIT Service
  Desk](https://oit.duke.edu/help/) or call **919-684-2200**. Include the exact
  error message and whether the license appears in Software Manager, but never
  send your password or an MFA code.
